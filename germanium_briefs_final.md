# Umicore (UMI · Euronext Brussels) — Brief

**WHERE THE MONEY IS** · Germanium Input Page · Stillpoint Intelligence

**Share price:** €17.26 (Apr 2026)
**Market cap:** ~€4.1B · **EV:** ~€5.5B · **EV/EBITDA:** 5.74x
**FY25:** €3.6B revenue, €847M adj. EBITDA, €558M Specialty Materials revenue
**Net debt:** €1.35B · **Leverage:** 1.60x
**52-week range:** €7.47 – €22.08

---

## What is this and why does it matter here?

Umicore operates the only facility in the western world that produces fiber-grade germanium tetrachloride at commercial scale. GeCl₄ is the volatile liquid deposited into glass preforms to control the refractive index of fiber optic cable — without it, light doesn't travel through fiber. Umicore's Olen, Belgium plant processes approximately 40-50 tonnes of germanium per year, refining it to 8N purity and shipping the resulting GeCl₄ to every major western and Japanese fiber manufacturer: Corning, Prysmian, Fujikura, Sumitomo Electric, Shin-Etsu.

Two other western entities produce GeCl₄ — Teck Resources at Trail and Indium Corporation — but neither operates at the scale or purity tier required for commercial fiber. China's export controls have made Umicore's position structural rather than discretionary. Umicore is the chokepoint.

The company is a diversified materials technology group — Catalysis (€450M EBITDA), Recycling (€371M EBITDA), Specialty Materials (€108M EBITDA), and troubled Battery Materials Solutions (-€21M EBITDA). The germanium business is buried inside Specialty Materials. The market prices Umicore as a struggling battery conglomerate at 5.74x EV/EBITDA. The germanium thesis is invisible in the headline numbers.

---

## How does value flow through this entity?

Three interlocking revenue channels create margins structurally wider than segment-level numbers suggest.

**Direct GeCl₄ sales** at prices linked to western germanium market pricing. With germanium at $8,597/kg — up from $1,340/kg in 2023 — this is the primary revenue driver.

**Closed-loop tolling.** Over 50% of Umicore's germanium input comes from recycled manufacturing scrap. The tolling fee is largely fixed regardless of spot price. When germanium surges, input cost on recycled material barely moves while output pricing follows the market. The customer cannot take scrap elsewhere — no other western facility can process it at required purity. Self-renewing revenue stream with expanding margins.

**The China-Belgium arbitrage.** Stimson Center documented that in 2024, US germanium imports from China fell by ~5,900 kg while Belgian imports rose by ~6,150 kg. Germanium is being routed through Belgium, purchased under license at Chinese domestic pricing (~$2,000/kg), processed at Olen, and sold at $7,000-8,500/kg. Umicore captures the 2-3x spread.

Blended cost structure: ~25-35% of input exposed to spot, ~65-75% insulated. But 100% of output tracks western pricing. Every price increase widens the spread.

**The GASIR® hedge.** Umicore's proprietary chalcogenide glass reduces germanium in IR optics by 80%. Over 1.5M lenses shipped. If germanium prices rise, Umicore profits from GeCl₄. If IR optics substitutes away from germanium, it's Umicore's technology doing the substituting.

---

## Key numbers

**Specialty Materials FY25:** €558M revenue (+4%), €108M adj. EBITDA (+11%). Germanium revenue "much higher" per management. Estimated $100-130M at FY25 pricing, potentially $345-430M+ at current elevated pricing.

**Foundation businesses combined:** €929M EBITDA. Sell-side SOTP: 7-9x implies €6.5-8.4B foundation EV vs €5.5B current group EV.

**DRC Big Hill:** Exclusive offtake. First concentrates Oct 2024. 30t/yr nameplate, targeting 30% of global supply. Additive to current 40-50t throughput.

**Balance sheet:** €1.55B cash, €1.35B net debt, 1.60x leverage. €524M free cash flow. €0.50/share dividend (~2.9% yield).

---

## What to watch

**Battery Materials resolution** — divestiture would trigger SOTP re-rating. JM precedent at 13.3x EV/EBITDA.

**DRC ramp-up pace** — each 10t adds ~$40-50M revenue.

**Olen capacity expansion** — any announcement would be material.

**Germanium price reversal risk** — if China relaxes controls, tolling model provides partial insulation but direct revenue compresses.

**Single-site concentration** — all western GeCl₄ through one facility.

---

## Investment angle

Umicore at 5.74x EV/EBITDA is the value play in the germanium chain. The market prices it as a battery conglomerate in distress. What the market misses: the foundation businesses (€929M EBITDA) are growing and cash-generative, and the germanium business is positioned at the most important chokepoint in the western supply chain.

The catalyst is Battery Materials resolution. If management divests or separates the division, foundation businesses get valued independently. A re-rating from 5.74x to 7-8x implies 25-40% equity upside before any germanium-specific revaluation.

The variant perception: most investors see a battery company with commodity exposure. The germanium angle is invisible because it's buried inside Specialty Materials. An investor who understands the GeCl₄ chokepoint, the tolling arbitrage, and the DRC ramp is seeing a business the sell-side isn't modeling correctly. Germanium revenue at current pricing could already approach the entire Specialty Materials segment total — but no analyst is publishing that estimate.

The €0.50/share dividend, €524M free cash flow, and 1.60x leverage provide downside cushion. Sell-side targets range €15.50-23.90. If germanium scarcity persists and Battery Materials resolves, this is the most asymmetric risk/reward in the chain.

Euronext Brussels-listed. Liquid for institutional investors.

---

## What is this and why does it matter here?

5N Plus is the only company in the western world that takes raw germanium and turns it into a finished space solar cell. The chain runs end to end: refined germanium metal → crystal → wafers → epitaxial III-V layers → completed multi-junction solar cell. This vertical integration lives across Montreal (refining), St. George, Utah (wafers), and Heilbronn, Germany (AZUR SPACE, cells).

AZUR SPACE, acquired in 2021, has delivered over 15 million space-qualified solar cells powering 700+ missions without a failure. Its cells power Hubble, Rosetta, Mars Express, Europa Clipper, JUICE, Chandrayaan-3, and more.

5N Plus also supplies germanium wafers and blanks for IR optics. It sits on a parallel branch of the supply chain from Umicore — neither competes with the other. Both pull on the same constrained supply.

---

## How does value flow through this entity?

**Space solar** is the growth engine. LEO mega-constellations have shattered the old rhythm of 20-30 GEO satellites per year. AZUR, alongside Spectrolab (Boeing) and SolAero (Rocket Lab), forms a three-player western oligopoly. Capacity expanding 25-35% annually, backlog maxed out.

**The SpaceX exception:** Starlink uses silicon cells, not III-V. Every other operator relies on germanium-based cells because they need maximum power per kg. For deep space, silicon isn't viable.

**DoD investment:** Two DPA Title III awards totaling $32.5M. The January 2026 award expands zone refining capacity sevenfold to 20+ tonnes/year at St. George, Utah — creating domestic germanium refining at scale.

---

## Key numbers

**FY25:** $391.1M revenue (+35%), $92.4M EBITDA (+73%). Net earnings $50.6M. Adjusted gross margin 33.7%.

**Specialty Semiconductors:** $285.4M revenue (+41%), $70.1M EBITDA (+59%). AZUR likely $100-130M of this.

**Backlog:** $394.9M — 353 days of annualized revenue.

**Balance sheet:** Net debt $50.3M, leverage 0.54x. Debt-free effectively. No dividend.

**FY26 guidance:** $100-105M EBITDA.

---

## What to watch

**AZUR capacity execution** — 25% additional in 2026 on top of 30% in 2025 and 35% in 2024.

**Silicon substitution risk** — if Kuiper or others follow SpaceX's silicon approach.

**Feedstock dependence** — 5N Plus doesn't control its own germanium supply. DoD refining investment addresses this but takes 2-3 years.

**CEO transition** — Perron assumes CEO from Jacques in May 2026.

---

## Investment angle

The thesis is validated — the question is whether 44x earnings leaves room. 5N Plus re-rated from C$5 to C$35 in twelve months.

For bulls: the backlog ($395M, 353 days) provides more visibility than the multiple implies. If EBITDA reaches $130-150M by FY27, the stock trades at ~17-20x forward — reasonable for a defense-backed semiconductor business with 30%+ organic growth.

For bears: SpaceX proved silicon works for mega-constellations. Feedstock dependence on Teck and Chinese imports is a vulnerability. At 44x, modest deceleration triggers a drawdown.

TSX-listed, NASDAQ cross-listed (BMM). No dividend. For investors who believe space solar demand has years to run and germanium scarcity persists, 5N Plus is the growth vehicle. For those who prefer a better entry, the backlog means patience is viable.

## What is this and why does it matter here?

LightPath is the germanium substitution play. The company holds an exclusive license from the US Naval Research Laboratory for BlackDiamond chalcogenide glass — a material that can replace germanium in infrared optics while delivering comparable or superior performance. IR optics accounts for roughly 24% of global germanium demand.

LightPath has transformed from a component supplier into a vertically integrated camera and imaging systems provider. The G5 Infrared acquisition (February 2025) added cooled IR camera manufacturing. LightPath now controls: proprietary glass → lens molding → optical coatings → finished camera assembly. China's export controls are the accelerant — every defense prime reassessing germanium supply is a potential BlackDiamond customer.

---

## How does value flow through this entity?

Revenue has shifted from individual lenses (low ASPs) to complete camera systems (high ASPs). The Assemblies and Modules segment grew 436% in Q1 FY26 to $5.86M, contributing 39% of revenue.

Three revenue layers: BlackDiamond components sold to other manufacturers; complete G5 camera systems for military, security, and public safety; and engineering qualification contracts (F-35, Navy SPEIR, unnamed defense customer) that create multi-year production tails.

The F-35 program validation is the most significant — BlackDiamond optics qualified on the world's highest-profile combat aircraft creates a reference design. The drone/UAV market is the emerging volume play, with $8M strategic investment from Ondas Holdings and Unusual Machines.

---

# Blue Moon Metals (MOON · TSXV / NASDAQ) — Brief

**WHERE THE MONEY IS** · Germanium Input Page · Stillpoint Intelligence

**Listed:** TSXV: MOON / NASDAQ: BMM
**Stage:** Pre-revenue, exploration/development
**Key asset:** Apex germanium-gallium mine, Washington County, Utah
**Acquired from:** Teck Resources, closed March 2026

---

## What is this and why does it matter here?

Blue Moon acquired the Apex mine — the only site in the United States with documented history of primary germanium and gallium production. Every other western source produces germanium as a by-product. Apex is where germanium and gallium were the primary target.

The mine operated in the 1980s-90s under Musto Explorations and Hecla Mining as the primary US germanium producer. It shut when prices made primary mining uneconomic. At today's $8,500+/kg, the same ore is worth 17x more per unit of contained germanium.

Blue Moon is building a western US "hub and spoke" platform: Apex (Ge/Ga) in Utah, Blue Moon mine (Zn/Cu/Au/Ag) in California with underground development underway, Springer metallurgical complex in Nevada for processing, and logistical connections to Teck's Trail smelter.

---

## How does value flow through this entity?

It doesn't yet — Blue Moon is pre-revenue. The value is in the resource, strategic positioning, and institutional backing.

A 2018 historical estimate: 1Mt at 0.087% Ge, 0.033% Ga, 1.8% Cu, 41 g/t Ag. Contained germanium: ~870 tonnes — significant against 230t/yr global production. Grades are 10-100x higher than most deposits. In-situ value comparable to 0.5 oz/t gold ore.

The plan: process Apex ore at the Springer complex in Nevada, with downstream refining at Teck's Trail smelter. Blue Moon also consolidated the surrounding Gage properties, controlling a 5+ km critical minerals belt.

Important caveat: historical estimates are not NI 43-101 compliant. Modern drilling, resource modeling, and technical reports required before these are bankable.

---

## Key numbers

**Resource:** 1Mt at 0.087% Ge (historical, not NI 43-101). Contained Ge: ~870t. Hecla 1989 feasibility: 230,200t at 0.100% Ge.

**Shareholders:** Oaktree Capital, Hartree Partners ($12B government stockpile partner), Teck Resources (8%), Wheaton Precious Metals, Altius Minerals, Baker Steel Resources Trust.

**Production target:** 2028. Pre-revenue, pre-feasibility.

---

## What to watch

**NI 43-101 resource estimate** — validates the deposit.

**Bulk sample and metallurgical test work** — validates the processing. Historical recovery problems need modern solutions.

**Blue Moon zinc mine** — if it reaches production first, validates execution and provides cash flow for Apex development.

**US policy environment** — DoD investments, critical minerals executive orders, Hartree's $12B stockpile program all create favorable backdrop.

---

## Investment angle

Blue Moon is a pre-revenue junior miner and should be evaluated accordingly. No modern resource estimate, no feasibility study, no production timeline with engineering backing.

The shareholder register is the strongest signal. Oaktree, Hartree, Teck, Wheaton, Altius, Baker Steel — this institutional syndicate does not typically back junior miners without significant due diligence. Their presence suggests sophisticated capital believes the resource is real and the development path is credible. It does not guarantee success.

If Apex reaches production at even the historical rate of 2.5t/yr germanium, revenue at current pricing (~$22M/yr from Ge alone, plus Cu/Ga/Ag credits) would be material against the current market cap. At 5-10t/yr, revenue potential is $43-85M — transformative for a junior miner.

TSXV and NASDAQ dual-listed. Highly speculative. Suitable only for investors comfortable with junior mining risk who want early-stage exposure to domestic US germanium supply. Position sizing should reflect pre-revenue, pre-feasibility status.

## What is this and why does it matter here?

Trail produces 8-10 tonnes/year. This feeds multiple downstream channels: 5N Plus for wafer production, defense contractors for military GeCl₄, and the broader western semiconductor supply chain. Teck holds 8% equity in Blue Moon Metals after vending the Apex mine — creating a pipeline from Blue Moon's future output to Trail's processing capability.

---

## How does value flow through this entity?

Germanium is a rounding error in Teck's financials — a by-product credit reducing Red Dog zinc unit costs. At $8,500/kg, 10 tonnes is worth $85M — not insignificant but small against Teck's billions in copper/zinc revenue.

Trail has been strategically shifting toward higher-value specialty metals, with smelter profits doubling year-over-year as the company increases germanium, indium, and silver processing while reducing primary zinc throughput.

---

## Key numbers

Trail Ge output: 8-10t/yr, expanding to 12-14t by 2027. Anglo Teck C$850M Trail investment commitment. Blue Moon equity: 8%. Red Dog mine life: to 2031.

---

## What to watch

---

## Investment angle

The monitoring value is high. Trail's germanium output is a leading indicator for western primary supply. Quarterly commentary on Trail specialty metals and Red Dog mine life is essential monitoring.

The Blue Moon equity stake (8%) is interesting optionality — if Apex develops, Trail is the natural processing destination.

For investors who own Teck for copper, germanium is a free option embedded in their position.

# Germanium Metal — Brief

**WHERE THE MONEY IS** · Germanium Input Page · Stillpoint Intelligence

**Global supply:** ~230 tonnes/yr
**China share:** ~83% of primary production
**Trading:** OTC only — no futures, no exchange

---

## What is this and why does it matter here?

Germanium metal is the raw material that feeds every other entity on this page. The price moved from $1,340/kg to $8,597/kg in three years — a supply-side dislocation from Chinese export controls on a structurally constrained market.

Supply runs at ~230t/yr: ~120t Chinese primary (export controlled), ~90t recycled, ~11t Russian (sanctioned). New sources (DRC, Blue Moon, Trail expansion) add tonnage over years, none resolving the constraint before 2028-2029.

---

## How does the market work?

No futures contract, no exchange listing, no standardized benchmark. All OTC. The 3.5x spread between Chinese domestic (~$2,000/kg) and western pricing ($7,000-8,500/kg) persists because export controls prevent arbitrage. Only entities with MOFCOM licenses and western processing relationships — primarily Umicore — can bridge the gap.

No way to hedge germanium exposure. Fiber manufacturers, defense contractors, and solar cell producers must accept spot pricing or negotiate long-term contracts. This creates strong customer loyalty to reliable suppliers.

Price discovery is slow and asymmetric — informational advantages exist for participants tracking the supply chain closely.

---

## What to watch

**November 2026 ban review** — the single most important event. Extension sustains thesis. Relaxation compresses it.

**Chinese domestic pricing** — rising domestic prices signal internal tightening. Stable prices suggest the premium is purely from controls.

**Western supply additions** — collectively 30-50t of potential new supply by decade's end. Meaningful but not transformative.

---

## Investment angle

Not directly investable without physical trading infrastructure. No futures, no ETF, no exchange. The only way to express a view on germanium pricing is through equities: Umicore (value play on price persistence), 5N Plus (growth play on downstream demand), LightPath (substitution play on price going too high).

The informational edge from tracking germanium pricing is real. An opaque OTC market with slow price discovery creates advantages for participants tracking supply chain flows — which is precisely what Stillpoint Intelligence provides.

The November 2026 ban review is the event every germanium-linked position should be stress-tested against. An investor should answer: "If the ban lifts and germanium reverts to $3,000/kg, which positions still work?" Umicore's tolling model provides partial insulation. 5N Plus's DoD funding and backlog provide demand-side protection. LightPath's thesis weakens significantly. Blue Moon becomes uneconomic. The answers aren't uniform — portfolio construction across these names matters more than any individual position.
